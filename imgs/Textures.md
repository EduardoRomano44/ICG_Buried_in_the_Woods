# Textures
Some textures were gathered from AmbientGC, while others were created on blender.

## [AmbientCG](https://ambientcg.com/)
- Grass006 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the ground.
- Stylized_Grass002 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of tree leafs.
- Bark014 - Used for Roughness and Normal Maps of the tree bark.
- Wood035 - Used for Roughness and Normal Maps of the table and basement entrance doors.
- Concrete030 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the basement entrance.
- Concrete035 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the basement ceiling, while also applying tiling.
- Concrete044 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the basement floor, while also aplying tiling.
- PaintedPlaster011, PaintedPlater009 and Planks37A - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the basement walls.
- Metal024 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of the basement metal door.

## Blender
- Color Map for bench planks, table and basement entrance doors.
- Color Map for the road.
- Normal Map for slime's outer layer.

## [3DTextures](https://3dtextures.me/)
- Stylized_Grass002 - Used for Color, Roughness and Normal Maps of tree leafs.
